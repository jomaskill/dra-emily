## Phase Goal

**As a** site owner, **I want to** know exactly what the site publishes and hold dated decision evidence, **so that** I can release only content that is safe and approved.

<objective>
Integrate every Phase 1 inventory, baseline, and evidence validator into one fail-closed release decision, require accountable human evidence, and prove that Phase 1 preserved public behavior.

Purpose: Turn independent governance records into the exact-revision release boundary promised by the roadmap without fabricating external decisions.
Output: ReleaseGate orchestration, integrated Pest coverage, a canonical release report, a blocking accountable-human checkpoint, and final before/after public-freeze validation.
</objective>

## Scope justification

These ten files form one atomic release-gate sequence. The five human registers are checkpoint inputs; `ReleaseGate`, the command, governance configuration, integration test, and release report must change together so readiness cannot be partial or bypassed. Splitting them would create an intermediate state where decisions are collected but not enforced, or where comparison is not bound to the same revision.
